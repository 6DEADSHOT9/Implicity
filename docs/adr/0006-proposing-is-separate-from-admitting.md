# Proposing is separate from admitting

Rules and Hermes may only produce proposals. One deterministic arbiter decides what is applied, and nothing else can change state. This lets the proposing side grow more inventive (new rules, Hermes drafting looks and widgets) without the system becoming less predictable, and it makes every change explainable and reversible. The old system had two paths to the interface, the policy engine and the agent's own proposal tool, which could conflict.
